Frequently Asked Questions

How should I prepare for my 3D Visia Diagnostic Scan?

Please arrive with clean skin free of heavy makeup, mineral sunscreen, or topical oils. This ensures 100% accurate multispectral UV porphyrin and melanin readings.

Are virtual tele-dermatology consultations available?

Yes. International clients can request a 45-minute virtual video assessment. You will receive a high-resolution photo kit to capture baseline images prior to your call.

What downtime is expected after picosecond laser or RF micro-needling?

Picosecond laser protocols carry zero social downtime (mild erythema resolves in 2–4 hours). Sub-dermal RF micro-needling requires 24 hours of barrier soothing serum application.

How far in advance should appointments be reserved?

In-person consultations with Dr. Vance and Dr. Mercer are typically booked 2 to 3 weeks in advance. Urgent post-procedure consultations are accommodated within 48 hours.
